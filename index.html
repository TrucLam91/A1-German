🌿 Nami Island Day Trip Guide 남이섬 · How to get there + 6 things to do
🚂 How to Get from Seoul to Nami Island
Total journey: ~90 min · Full day trip recommended · Depart Seoul by 8–9 AM
Option A — By Train (Most Popular)
1
Seoul → Gapyeong Station (가평역)
Take the ITX-Cheongchun Train from Yongsan Station (near Seoul Station, Hongdae side) or Cheongnyangni Station (near Dongdaemun side). Journey: ~55–60 min. Runs every ~30 min. Book online at KORAIL (korail.com) or Klook — weekends sell out fast!
₩5,900–6,400 one-way (~€3.4–3.7)
2
Gapyeong Station → Gapyeong Wharf (선착장)
From Gapyeong Station, take the Gapyeong City Tour Bus (hop-on hop-off, ₩6,000 all day) or a taxi (₩6,900, ~5 min). Bus route covers Nami Island + Petite France + Garden of Morning Calm. Walk is ~15 min via a scenic riverside path if you prefer.
Bus: ₩6,000 (~€3.5) · Taxi: ₩6,900 (~€4.0)
3
Gapyeong Wharf → Nami Island
Choose between ferry (5 min, departs every 30 min) or zipline/zip-wire (1 min, spectacular aerial entry). Both include Nami Island entry fee.
Ferry round-trip: ₩16,000 (~€9.3) · Zipline one-way: ₩65,000 (~€37.9)
Option B — Direct Shuttle Bus from Seoul (Hassle-Free)
🚌
Direct shuttle from Hongdae / Myeongdong / Dongdaemun
Shuttle buses operate daily from major Seoul tourist areas direct to Nami Island ferry terminal. Book via Klook or KKday. Includes return. No transfers, air-conditioned, door-to-door.
₩30,000–45,000 round-trip including ferry (~€17–26)
💡 Best combo for 1 day: ITX train → City Tour Bus (₩6,000 covers all stops) → Nami Island ferry → explore island → Petite France → Garden of Morning Calm → train back. Budget ~₩40,000–50,000/person for transport + entry.

🕐 Daily 08:00 – 21:00 ★ 4.5 / 5 43K reviews · Google Maps Free w/ Entry🔥 TikTok Viral
A cathedral-like avenue of towering metasequoia trees stretching the length of the island. Every season transforms the atmosphere — spring pink blossoms, summer deep green canopy, autumn golden-red leaves, winter snow-dusted branches. Featured in the hit K-drama 'Winter Sonata' (2002). The Winter Sonata statue at Gongsaengwon Garden is nearby.
💡 Walk the full length slowly — it's only ~500m but every angle is a photo. Visit early morning (before 10AM) for the most ethereal misty light and fewer people.

Riverside Ginkgo & Maple Lanes
강변 은행나무 & 단풍나무길
Autumn foliage capital — brilliant gold & red in October–November
🕐 Daily 08:00 – 21:00 ★ 4.5 / 5 43K reviews · Google Maps Free w/ Entry🔥 TikTok Viral
Multiple tree-lined paths along the island's riverbanks — Lover's Ginkgo Tree Lane turns blazing yellow in autumn, while the Maple Lane explodes with red and orange. Walking the riverside wooden path with mountain reflections on the water is one of Korea's most beautiful experiences.
💡 Autumn (mid-Oct to mid-Nov) is peak season — the whole island glows. June is lush green and uncrowded. Bring a picnic blanket and sit by the river.

Ferry & Zipline Entry Experience
페리 & 짚라인 (입장 방법)
Two dramatic ways to arrive — ferry scenic, zipline thrilling
🕐 Ferry: Daily 07:30 – 21:30 · Zipline: Daily 09:00 – 17:30 ★ 4.4 / 5 8.1K reviews · Google Maps 🔥 TikTok Viral
The 5-minute ferry crossing from Gapyeong Wharf offers panoramic mountain-and-river views as you approach the island. The zipline (Nami Skyline Zip Wire) sends you flying over the Han River at speed — arriving on the island from above. The zipline is one of the longest in Korea at 940m. Book zipline on-site only (no advance booking), so arrive early.
💡 Take the zipline INTO the island and ferry OUT — best of both. Zipline queue can be 1–2 hours on weekends, so arrive at Gapyeong Wharf before 9AM if you want to zipline.

Island Cycling
섬 자전거 타기
Cycle all paths around the island in 30–45 minutes
🕐 Bicycle rental: Daily 09:00 – 18:00 ★ 4.3 / 5 2.4K reviews · Google Maps
The entire island is only about 4km in circumference — perfect for a leisurely bicycle loop. Bike rentals are available near the ferry dock. The tree-canopied paths make cycling here feel completely different from city cycling. Tandems and solo bikes available.
💡 Rent a tandem bicycle for a romantic ride through the metasequoia and ginkgo paths. ₩10,000/hr for tandem, ₩7,000/hr for solo.

Petite France
쁘띠 프랑스 · Nearby Attraction
French village theme park — pastel buildings on a hillside
🕐 Daily 09:00 – 18:00 ★ 4.0 / 5 12K reviews · Google Maps 🔥 TikTok Viral
A French-inspired theme park about 10 minutes from Nami Island via the City Tour Bus. Colorful pastel buildings set on a hillside overlooking a lake. Features The Little Prince displays, traditional French puppet shows, and photogenic architecture. Appeared in Korean dramas 'My Love from the Star' and 'Secret Garden'.
💡 Combine with Nami Island on the same day using the City Tour Bus (₩6,000). Go to Petite France in the afternoon after Nami Island.

Garden of Morning Calm
아침고요수목원 · Nearby Attraction
Korea's most beautiful botanical garden — especially at night
🕐 Daily 08:30 – 18:00 · Night Illumination: Seasonal (17:00 – 21:00) ★ 4.5 / 5 14K reviews · Google Maps 🔥 TikTok Viral
One of Korea's most beloved botanical gardens, about 20 min from Nami Island by City Tour Bus. 13 themed gardens across rolling hillside terrain. The seasonal night illumination festival (usually held in winter and spring) transforms the entire garden into a magical carpet of lights.
💡 Check if night illumination is running during your June visit — it's worth timing your day trip for it. The Morning Garden (오전 정원) and Korean Traditional Garden are the most beautiful sections.
